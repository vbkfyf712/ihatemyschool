Free style? Forget it. Dressing for the weather or the way you feel comfortable? Yeah, roll your lip back in. A robot must be in its shell, so we put on our jackets and don't rebel.

For those who want to go here, I only have my condolences because half of my class is unhinged. And so am I. Just a little bit.
